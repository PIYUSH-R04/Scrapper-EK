1. await page.goto("https://ekantipur.com/entertainment", wait_until="domcontentloaded").
2. await page.wait_for_selector("div.category-inner-wrapper", state="attached").
3. Read the page-level category label once: text of ".category-name a" inside <header>.
4. cards = await page.locator("div.category-inner-wrapper").all()
5. Iterate the first 5 cards. For each card, in its own try/except:

     IMPORTANT: data-src, not src. Images are lazy-loaded.
   - Build Article(title, image_url, category=<page label>, author).
6. On exception, log.warning with card index and exception; skip that card; do not crash.
7. Return the list.
